notice

This is to inform all students, parents, and faculty members that our school will be celebrating its 25th Annual Cultural Fest, "Utsav 2026," on Saturday, 12 December 2026, starting at 4:00 PM in the Main School Auditorium. The event will feature classical and contemporary dance performances, theatrical plays, musical ensembles, and the annual prize distribution ceremony honoring academic and co-curricular achievers. Auditions and rehearsals for participating students will begin next week during the zero period and after school hours. All interested students must register their names with their respective House In-charges by 20 November 2026. Students participating in stage events are required to submit a signed parental consent letter prior to the start of rehearsals. Attendance is mandatory for all students on the day of the event, and everyone must report to the campus in neat, complete school uniform by 3:15 PM.This is to inform all students, parents, and faculty members that our school will be celebrating its 25th Annual Cultural Fest, "Utsav 2026," on Saturday, 12 December 2026, starting at 4:00 PM in the Main School Auditorium.
